Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

CarPrice respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the CarPrice mobile application and how that information is used.

1. Introduction

CarPrice ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use the CarPrice mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

CarPrice does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The application processes the following information:

  • Vehicle photographs: Images are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • License plate numbers: Once recognized, the vehicle registration number is transmitted to our server to retrieve vehicle information and estimated market prices.

3. How We Use Information

The vehicle registration number is used to:

  • Identify the vehicle.
  • Retrieve available vehicle information.
  • Provide estimated vehicle market prices.
  • Display regional price statistics.

4. Data Storage and Security

Vehicle photographs remain on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

License plate numbers are transmitted over a secure HTTPS connection. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect information during transmission and processing.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of CarPrice does not use:

  • Google AdMob or other advertising services.
  • Firebase Analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics.

The application does not display advertisements.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Vehicle registration numbers may be processed by our server to provide the application's functionality. We do not intentionally share this information with third parties for advertising purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

CarPrice is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
